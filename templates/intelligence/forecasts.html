How forecasting works

  1. Daily sales per product are built with zero-sales days included. Days that started or ended out of stock are excluded, because sales then understate true demand.
  2. Each candidate model is trained on history up to {{ run.holdout_days }} days ago and scored on the following {{ run.holdout_days }} days it has never seen (no future data leaks into training).
  3. The model with the lowest WAPE is chosen per product (ties go to the simpler model) and retrained on all history.
  4. Features: weekday, payday period, Rwandan public holidays, season, trend and recent demand level.
  5. Forecast intervals (80%) come from each model's holdout errors and feed the safety-stock calculation.
